Fixture resize handles

When a fixture is selected, how do you resize it?

A

Corner + edge handles (Figma-style)

Selected fixture shows 8 handles: 4 corners (drag to resize proportionally or freely) and 4 edge midpoints (drag to resize on one axis only). Rotation handle appears above the fixture.

B

Edge-only handles (simpler)

Selected fixture shows only 4 edge-midpoint handles (top/bottom/left/right). Drag an edge to change width or depth. Simpler gesture model — no ambiguity between corner resize and move. Rotation stays on long-press action sheet.

C

No drag handles — resize via the mini-panel

No on-canvas resize handles. The selection mini-panel shows editable width/depth fields. Type the new dimensions and the fixture updates. Simpler canvas, no accidental resizes, but less tactile.
