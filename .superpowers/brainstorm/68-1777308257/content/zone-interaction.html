How should zones be moved and edited?

Currently you can only drag vertices. v0.25 adds whole-zone movement — which model feels right?

A

Two-mode: Select → Move or Edit

Tap a zone to select it. Selected zone shows two controls: a move handle (drag from center icon to reposition the whole zone) and vertex handles (drag corners to reshape). Tapping empty space deselects.

Drag ⊕ to move · Drag circles to reshape
B

Figma-style: drag interior = move, drag vertex = reshape

Tap to select. Drag from inside the zone polygon = moves whole zone. Drag a vertex = reshapes. No separate move handle — the behavior changes based on where you touch.

Dashed = selected · white circles = reshape handles
C

Toolbar mode toggle: Move vs Edit Shape

A small toolbar (or FAB) lets you toggle between Move Mode (drag anywhere on zone to reposition it) and Edit Mode (vertex handles appear to reshape). Clear, explicit, no ambiguity.

MOVE
SHAPE

I lean toward B (Figma-style) — it's the most intuitive once you understand it, and matches what designers expect. But A is more explicit and easier to discover for non-designers.
