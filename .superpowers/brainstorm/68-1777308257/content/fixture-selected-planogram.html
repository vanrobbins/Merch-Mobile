Selected fixture — how do you see the planogram?

Tap-to-select already works. Badge tap = assign/change. But the user should also see planogram info on selection.

A

Selection mini-panel (recommended)

Selecting a fixture slides up a compact panel from the bottom of the canvas (not full-screen). Shows fixture name, dimensions, and the assigned planogram with a View → button that navigates to the full PlanogramDetailScreen.

B

Badge expands on selection

When the fixture is selected, its planogram badge expands in-place to show the planogram name + slot summary + a View button. No panel — everything stays on the canvas. Deselecting collapses it back to the small badge.

C

Combined: badge tap = assign, selection = mini-panel + inline badge

Both at once: the badge is always visible (and tappable to assign), AND selecting shows the mini-panel at the bottom with full planogram detail + View button. Most information visible, most surface area used.

I recommend A — the mini-panel is clean, readable, and doesn't clutter the canvas. The badge still handles assign/change. C is equally good if you want both simultaneously.
